## What AGPL-3.0 requires

service that other people can interact with, you must offer those 
users the source code of your modifications under AGPL-3.0.

This applies whether you charge for the service or not. It applies 
to public services, hosted services, and SaaS deployments. It does 
NOT apply to internal-only deployments inside your organization.

## Why AGPL-3.0

- **Read the source** — verify security claims, audit the code, 
      understand what it does with your data
- **Run it yourself** — self-host on your own infrastructure with 
      no vendor lock-in
- **Trust through transparency** — the hosted service runs the same 
      code you can read on GitHub
