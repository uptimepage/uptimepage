Monitors can now be checked from São Paulo, Brazil. It is our first probe region in South America, alongside San Jose, New York, Frankfurt, Helsinki and Singapore.

**Opt-in.** New monitors still start in New York, Frankfurt and Helsinki. Tick São Paulo on the monitor form to add it, or pass `sa-br` in `regions` through the REST API. Existing monitors keep their regions until you add it.

**Plans.** Standard runs a monitor from up to three regions and Founding from up to five, any you choose. Pro and Team have no region cap. Past your plan's count, adding São Paulo means unticking another.

**History.** Results from São Paulo are stored per region like every other, so the per-region latency chart and the region filter on the monitor page pick it up straight away.
