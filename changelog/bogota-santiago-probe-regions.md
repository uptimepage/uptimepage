Monitors can now be checked from Bogotá and Santiago. With São Paulo, that makes three probe regions in South America: Bogotá is closest to Colombia and the north of the continent, Santiago to Chile and the Pacific coast. The hosted service now checks from thirteen regions.

**Opt-in.** New monitors still start in New York, Frankfurt and Helsinki. Tick Bogotá or Santiago on the monitor form, or pass `sa-co` or `sa-cl` in `regions` through the REST API. Existing monitors keep their regions until you add one.

**Plans.** Standard runs a monitor from up to three regions and Founding from up to five, any you choose. Pro and Team have no region cap.

Docs: [probe regions](/docs/hosted/regions).
