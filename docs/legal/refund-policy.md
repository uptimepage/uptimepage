# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-23

This policy explains when you can get your money back for a paid uptimepage plan. It restates what our [Terms of Service](/terms) say, and the Terms win if the two ever differ.

## Who handles refunds

Paid plans are sold by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for every order. Paddle takes the payment and issues every refund, to the payment method you used. Paddle's own [refund policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) also applies.

## 14 days to change your mind

If you are a consumer, you can withdraw from a new subscription within 14 days of buying it, without giving a reason, and get a full refund, taxes included, within 14 days of your notice. Your organisation returns to its free plan.

To withdraw, use any of these:

- the withdrawal button in Paddle's customer portal, reached from the "Manage subscription" link in your order confirmation email or at <https://paddle.net>
- a refund request at <https://paddle.net>
- an email to [hello@uptimepage.dev](mailto:hello@uptimepage.dev) with your account email and order date

## After the first 14 days

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your plan stays in place until the end of the period you already paid for, and that period is not refunded. You can cancel any time in [billing settings](https://app.uptimepage.dev/settings/billing).

We refund the unused part of the current period when:

- we reduce the limits of a paid plan you hold and you cancel because of it
- we make a material change to the Terms and you cancel before it takes effect

Nothing here limits a refund you are owed under the law of the country where you live.

## Free plans

Standard and Founding cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
