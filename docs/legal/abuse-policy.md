# Abuse Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-23

We take reports of abuse seriously. This page describes what counts as
abuse, how to report it, and what we'll do. It is also how we handle
notices of illegal content under the EU Digital Services Act.

## What Counts as Abuse

We consider the following abusive use of the uptimepage service:

- **Unauthorised monitoring:** Configuring targets pointing at systems
  the operator does not own or have permission to monitor
- **DDoS amplification:** Configuring high-frequency checks against a
  victim's infrastructure
- **Illegal content:** Hosting illegal content on a public status page
  (e.g., copyright violations, defamation, illegal-in-jurisdiction speech)
- **Trademark infringement:** Squatting on an organisation slug or
  public name that infringes a registered trademark
- **Malware or phishing:** Using public status pages to distribute
  malware or facilitate phishing
- **Harassment:** Targeting individuals through targeted misinformation
  on a public page
- **Resource abuse:** Repeatedly bypassing quota mechanisms; creating
  multiple accounts to circumvent free-tier limits

## How to Report

Email **[hello@uptimepage.dev](mailto:hello@uptimepage.dev)** with subject line `[ABUSE]` and include:

1. The exact URL of the offending content or organisation slug
2. Why you believe it is illegal or abusive (categories above), in enough
   detail for us to see it without a legal investigation
3. Evidence (screenshots, logs, links)
4. Your name and email address (we may need to follow up)
5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the information in
   your report is accurate and complete
6. If reporting a trademark issue, proof of ownership

Items 4 and 5 are not needed for reports of child sexual abuse material,
which you may send anonymously.

We do not require you to identify yourself for other reports either, but
an anonymous report may not have the full effect of a notice under the
Digital Services Act.

## What Happens After You Report

We aim to:

- **Confirm receipt of your report within two business days**
- **Complete initial investigation within 7 days**
- **Take action and tell you our decision within 14 days**, with how you
  can challenge it

A person reviews every report. We do not decide reports by automated means.

For severe cases (active malware distribution, ongoing DDoS), we act
within hours.

If a report shows that a criminal offence involving a threat to someone's
life or safety has taken place, is taking place or is likely to, we
promptly inform the law enforcement or judicial authorities of the
country concerned.

## Actions We May Take

Depending on severity and evidence:

- **Warning:** Email to the account owner asking them to remedy
- **Content removal or restriction:** The content is taken down or its
  public page is disabled
- **Temporary suspension:** Account is suspended pending response
- **Permanent removal:** Account is terminated; its data is deleted after
  the 30 days our [Terms of Service](/terms) give you to take it
- **Slug forfeiture:** A squatted slug is returned to the rightful holder
- **Law enforcement referral:** For criminal activity, we cooperate with
  competent authorities

## Our Reasons

When we remove or restrict content, or suspend or close an account, we
email the account owner a statement of reasons, unless the content is
deceptive high-volume spam or an authority tells us not to. It says:

- what we did, what it covers and, for a suspension, how long it lasts
- the facts we relied on, and whether it was prompted by a report
- the law the content breaks, or the part of our
  [Terms of Service](/terms) or this policy it breaks, and why
- how to challenge the decision: an appeal to us, an out-of-court dispute
  settlement body certified under the Digital Services Act, or the courts

## Appeals

If we act on your content or account, or reject your report, you can
appeal by replying to our email within six months. A person who did not
take the original decision reviews it within 14 days, and we tell you the
outcome and the reasons.

## Trademark Disputes

If you believe an organisation slug or public name infringes your
trademark:

1. Email [hello@uptimepage.dev](mailto:hello@uptimepage.dev) with subject `[TRADEMARK]`
2. Include proof of trademark registration (registration number, country)
3. Identify the offending slug or organisation
4. State your relationship to the trademark holder

We will notify the current holder of the dispute. If they don't respond
within 14 days, or if the evidence is clear, we transfer the slug.

We do **not** mediate disputes where both parties have legitimate claims;
those need to be settled outside our process.

## Privacy

We treat abuse reports as confidential. We do not reveal the identity of
the reporter to the reported account holder unless legally required.
