heartbeat check

Reverses the direction: your cron job, backup script, or worker pings a unique URL on every run. No ping within the period (plus grace) opens an incident: a dead-man's-switch for things we can't reach.

manual check

For a service nothing can probe: a SIP trunk, a carrier link, a partner's back office. It starts up, and you set its state on the monitor page. Down or degraded opens an incident and shows on its status pages like any other monitor, and up closes it.

dns check

Resolves a record and (optionally) asserts a substring appears in the answer. NXDOMAIN or an empty answer set fails the check.

tls certificate check

Connects to host:port, fetches the served certificate, and warns before expiry. Degraded under warn_days, Down under critical_days.
